Who this notice covers

This notice applies to the ZAXWEIR company website at zaxweir.com. ZAXWEIR is an independent software venture operated by its founder. Contact: founder@zaxweir.com.

It covers this informational website. It does not describe data handling by future ORVARDEN software or other future products; those will need their own notices when available.

Browsing the website

The website does not have accounts, contact forms, payment processing, analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or embedded third-party media. The site’s code does not set cookies or use local storage to track visitors. Fonts, styles, and scripts are served with the website.

The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as your IP address, browser information, requested page, and request time, to deliver the website, maintain availability, and prevent abuse. GitHub Pages is the selected hosting service. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. Its handling of personal information is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Contacting us by email

The email link opens your own email application. Clicking it does not submit a form to this website. If you send an email, we receive your email address, message, and any details you choose to include. We use this information to respond and manage the conversation.

Email may pass through our domain email-forwarding provider, Namecheap, and Gmail, our receiving email service. Those providers process information under their own policies. See Namecheap’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy. Please do not send credentials or sensitive personal information.

Use, sharing, and retention

We use enquiry information to communicate with you. We do not sell enquiry information or use it for targeted advertising. Service providers may process it to deliver email and host the website, and we may disclose information when required by law.

We keep correspondence as needed to respond, maintain relevant business records, and meet applicable obligations. We do not promise a fixed retention period for provider logs or email backups, which are also governed by the providers’ policies.

Your choices

You can browse without sending any information through a form or creating an account. To ask about personal information in our correspondence or request correction or deletion, email founder@zaxweir.com. We will consider requests under applicable law and any recordkeeping obligations.

Changes to this notice

We will update this page if the website’s data practices change. The date above shows the most recent revision.
