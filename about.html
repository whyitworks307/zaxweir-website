01WHY WE’RE BUILDING

Good answers matter.
Useful action matters more.

AI can suggest how to organize files or finish a task. Having it actually help on your Windows PC is harder. You need a connection you can understand, permission you can control, and a way to see what was done.

That is the problem we want to solve. We’re developing the bridge between supported AI assistants and the computer you already use, with specific, reviewable actions instead of a blank check.
