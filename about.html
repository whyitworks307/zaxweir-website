01WHY WE’RE BUILDING

Asking AI is easy.
Getting useful work done is harder.

AI can explain how to organize a folder or complete a task. But acting on your own Windows computer is another matter. The connection needs to be useful, understandable, and under your direction.

That’s where ZAXWEIR is focused. We’re working on the bridge between an AI assistant and the computer you already use—without treating broad, unrestricted access as the default.
