01WHY WE’RE BUILDING

AI can tell you how.
We want it to help you do it.

It’s easy to ask an AI assistant how to organize files. Getting it to help on your actual Windows PC is another story. You need to know what it can access and what it did.

We started with that gap. Our first product is intended to connect supported AI assistants to your computer, one permitted task at a time. Not with blanket access.
