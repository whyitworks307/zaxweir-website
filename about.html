ABOUT ZAXWEIR

Technology should
answer to people.

ZAXWEIR is a founder-led, independent software venture focused on practical tools for computer control and automation.

OUR VISION

Give people a clearer way
to work with their technology.

Managing technology should not become a second job. Our aim is to make computer tasks easier to direct, understand, and fit into everyday life.

We are building ZAXWEIR from the ground up, beginning with ORVARDEN. The product is still under development, and we will describe its availability as that work progresses.

OUR TECHNOLOGY FOCUS

Control.
Coordination.
Clarity.

01

Practical computer control

Tools that help people work with their computers beyond the desk.

02

Thoughtful automation

Routine work guided by the person using it, with a clear purpose for each action.

03

Understandable software

Interfaces that explain what is happening and make the next step clear.
