CURRENT STATUS

In development.

ORVARDEN has been used internally, but it is not ready for public sign-up, download, or purchase. We’re working on secure account and device connections, straightforward installation, permissions, and reliability before opening it to others.

A release date and pricing have not been announced. The ORVARDEN public name remains provisional.

Your instructions. Clear boundaries.

The intended product experience, not a set of features available today.

  1. Choose an AI connection

    Start with a supported connection, subject to testing and provider restrictions.

  2. Connect your Windows PC

    Guided setup for your own computer is planned for release.

  3. Set permissions

    Choose the scope and approve actions when required.

  4. Review the outcome

    Check results, pause tasks, or revoke access.
