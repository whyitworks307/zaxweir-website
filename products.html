ORVARDEN.

Remote computer control
& task coordination

ORVARDEN is ZAXWEIR’s first commercial software product, currently under development. Its focus is helping people operate their computers remotely and coordinate routine tasks.

The development priorities below describe the direction of the product. They are not a list of publicly available features.

01 / CONTROL

Direct your computer

Make remote computer work easier to initiate and follow.

02 / COORDINATION

Bring tasks together

Help routine actions fit into a clear, manageable workflow.

03 / CLARITY

See what happens

Prioritize understandable permissions and visible action outcomes.
